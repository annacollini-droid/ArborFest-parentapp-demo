# Decision Log

[2026-06-08] Demo realism — Book & Pay content varies by child (school-level clubs, year-specific trips, per-child bookings)
Decision: For the upcoming demo, Book & Pay no longer shows Molly's content for every child. The model mirrors how Arbor actually works: clubs are a school-level catalogue (untagged → whole-school, so siblings at one school share them), trips/residentials carry a `years` tag and show only to that year group, and bookings (Needs attention / Upcoming / Past / landing "Next up") filter per child. Implemented via one `childBrowseItems` filter (`item.school` defaulting to "Oakwood Primary", plus `years` membership) and a `.filter(i => i.child === selectedChild.name)` on each booking array. Year groups (Molly Y4, Lucas Y6, Ethan Y9) are internal filtering keys only — never surfaced in the parent UI (parents know their child's year), and new club/trip titles are kept clean of year/school labels (e.g. "Leavers' residential – PGL Liddington", not "Year 6 …"). Molly stays the full test-bed (all payment-model and picker variants); Lucas and Ethan get enough plausible content to feel real — Molly & Lucas share the Oakwood Primary club list, Ethan gets a separate Riverside Secondary catalogue (Football, Debate, STEM & robotics, Basketball, Senior choir, Drama; NHM / National Theatre / DofE Bronze trips), with new clubs given full `clubExtras` + detail-whitelist entries so they open and book. Pre-existing consent notices that hardcoded a clashing year (Lucas "Year 2", Ethan "Year 10") had the year reference stripped rather than renumbered.
Rationale: Switching child only changed the name while clubs/trips stayed identical, which read as a hollow prototype in a demo. Filtering clubs by school (not child) is correct, not lazy — siblings at one school genuinely see the same club catalogue; only year-specific trips and per-child enrolments should differ. Stripping the stale year references (over renumbering) both removes the contradiction and respects the "don't surface year to the parent" rule, and avoids inventing unrealistic assignments (e.g. Year 9 work experience). Considered a lighter "bookings-only" split and an "Ethan-secondary-only" split; rejected both — the browse list is the most-clicked demo surface, so it had to differ too. Full per-child detail data (vs. a generic fallback) was chosen so drilling into Lucas/Ethan items doesn't break mid-demo.

[2026-06-08] Payments — pay-button + amount-sheet copy is consistent across Clubs and Trips
Decision: One vocabulary for every payment CTA and amount picker, applied identically in Clubs and Trips. (1) Primary/footer CTAs read "Pay now · £xx.xx"; "Pay in full · £xx.xx" is used only where a partial alternative sits beside it (instalment trips, owed-balance bookings). (2) The "choose amount" bottom sheets all confirm with "Pay now · £xx.xx" (the £0 voluntary case keeps "Confirm with £0"). (3) Sheet subtitle states the valid range proactively: min > 0 → "Pay £{min}–£{max} today" (e.g. Clubs "Pay £10–£50 today", trip instalment "Pay £85–£185 today"); min £0 (voluntary) → "Pay what you want, including £0". (4) Sheet titles: "Choose amount" when a minimum applies, "Choose your contribution" when voluntary. (5) Inline errors: "Minimum is £x" / "Maximum is £x" (plus the £2 Stripe-floor message on voluntary). The Stripe/Apple Pay gateway-sheet buttons ("Pay", "Pay £x.xx" with the lock) are deliberately left mimicking the provider and are out of this scheme.
Rationale: The range subtitle is not redundant with the "Minimum is £x" error or the prior detail screen — the error is reactive (fires only after going below) and the detail page is no longer in view, whereas the subtitle states both bounds up front, including the upper bound the error never mentions, which is the whole point of "pay a different amount". The fix for Trips' wordier "Minimum deposit £85 · Total £185" was therefore to adopt the existing Clubs range form rather than delete the line. Net effect: a parent sees the deposit floor on the detail page, "£85–£185 today" reaffirms it in the sheet, and "Minimum is £85" only fires if they push below. Single source of truth for payment phrasing reduces drift as either area changes.

[2026-06-08] Trips — deposit is the securing threshold; no "reserved—pending" state; pay deposit or in full
Decision: For instalment trips, the deposit IS the auto-confirmation threshold — they are one number, not two. Paying the deposit (or more) instantly Confirms the child's place; the remaining balance is tracked purely as an outstanding debt, never as a pending/at-risk state for the seat. The prototype was corrected accordingly: removed the separate `autoConfirmThreshold` field, removed the "Place reserved — pending" header/amber-clock/note from BookingConfirmedScreen, so deposit-cleared now reads "Booking confirmed". The deposit is a hard floor — an in-app booking cannot be completed for £0 to "reserve and pay later" (financial-hardship £0 reservations are a manual office/MIS bypass). Parents can pay any amount from the deposit up to the full total at booking (the deposit is just the minimum the checkout accepts), so the booking step offers both "Pay in full" and "Pay deposit", and the detail's cost box presents the total as the headline with the deposit as the minimum, not as the default.
Rationale: Confirmed against Arbor Help Centre mechanics ("Once the amount paid reaches the auto-confirmation threshold, the student will have paid the deposit and have their place confirmed"). The prototype had modelled deposit (£85) and threshold (£100) as different, inventing a "reserved—pending" limbo that does not exist in Arbor and contradicted the rule that a paid deposit secures the place. Three true states only: Eligible → Confirmed (outstanding balance) → Confirmed (fully paid). Known back-office trap to guard server-side (not a parent-app surface): the threshold must be ≥ the minimum instalment amount, or the parent is blocked from paying just the deposit (cf. PRB-369). Worth confirming exact contract with the Payments/Voyager team and testing comprehension on the panel ("after paying a deposit, is your child's place secured?").

[2026-06-08] Trips — consent is implied at booking; no in-app decline or cancellation
Decision: Trips collect consent the same way as Clubs — implied by the act of booking, surfaced as microcopy above the CTA ("By booking, you consent to your child attending this trip."). No explicit consent step, no checkbox gate, and no "decline / won't be attending" action anywhere in the flow. No parent-side cancellation either: once consented/paid the booking is locked; the parent contacts the office and staff remove/refund manually in the MIS. The app only ever displays the resulting state (e.g. Cancelled, Refunded). The booking flow has no separate consent page and no review/payment page — the trip detail is the whole flow (detail → pay/confirm → confirmation).
Rationale: Arbor has no "Decline Trip" option for parents by design. Optional trips treat no-action-by-deadline as implicit non-attendance; compulsory-trip declines are collected outside the Trips module (in-app message or a custom "Declining X" consent); changed-mind cancellations are office-handled. Aligning trips to the established Clubs implied-consent pattern keeps the flows consistent and avoids building a decline/cancel surface the SIS doesn't support. Sign-up deadlines are hard cut-offs (coach seating, insurance, safeguarding ratios), which is why the deadline earns prominent treatment when looming.

[2026-04-30] Error state patterns — system-wide rule
Decision: Three distinct error patterns are used based on context: (1) Toast — for errors on write actions (submit, send, report). Used when the form/content is still present behind the notification and the user can retry the action. (2) In-content error state — for load/fetch failures (inbox, sent list, individual message body). Replaces the content area with an icon, context-appropriate copy ("No connection — check your signal and try again" vs "Something went wrong — please try again"), and a Try again button. (3) Full-screen success — for positive completion outcomes only (e.g. absence reported). Never used for errors. Offline and server errors always produce different copy but use the same layout treatment.
Rationale: Toast auto-dismisses and is appropriate feedback for a user-initiated action — the parent knows what they tried to do. When content fails to load, toast leaves the user with a blank screen and no explanation. An in-content error state occupies the same visual slot the content would have filled, making the failure state unambiguous. Full-screen success is reserved for positive endpoints so it retains its meaning as a clear completion signal.

[2026-06-05] Icon semantics — three-tier system (door / label / none)
Decision: Icons across the app follow a three-tier semantic system. Now: X/scrim = back off (stay on the now-full detail, the A full-state), footer "Browse other clubs" = forward to the list.
Rationale: Anna flagged that the race sheet's icon matched nothing else and that no existing sheet uses a footer button without a top-right close — i.e. it was inventing chrome instead of joining a pattern. The top-up flow already sets a clean precedent (grabber + close-X + centred title + footer CTA, no icon), so codifying that as THE bottom-sheet identity makes every sheet legible and removes per-sheet guesswork. The X-vs-footer "two different jobs" rule avoids a redundant double-dismiss and gives the parent a real forward action. Captured in [[reference_overlay_pattern_system]] for reuse.

[2026-06-10] Full-state price for multi-option clubs — one "From £X" summary, never a grey stack of the option cards
Decision: A full club shows exactly ONE price summary. Single-option → the club-card box ("Full term" / membership / price). Multi-option → a single "From £X" line (mirrors the browse card's representation), not one grey box per period.
Rationale: When a multi-option club became full, the full-state rendered each period as a grey cost box — i.e. the selectable radio cards turned into a stack of grey boxes, which reads as a *disabled* state (the thing we avoid app-wide). A single grey box is fine for a genuinely single-option club (it matches that club's normal look), but a greyed stack mimicking the now-irrelevant options is wrong. Collapsing multi-option to one "From £X" line keeps price context (per the full-states "don't lose sight of the price" goal) without the disabled-cards read, and reuses the browse card's existing multi-option price form. Surfaced by Anna testing the paid race (Football, multi-option) landing on the full-state after the "just filled up" sheet.

[2026-06-10] Clubs — cost-box harmonisation with trips (name leads, basis qualifies, bare amount)
Decision: The single-option cost box and the multi-option radio cards both now lead with the school's membership name (primary line) and carry the inferred booking basis ("Full term" / "Per session") as the secondary line, with a bare amount on the right. The old right-side unit suffix ("total" / "each") is removed everywhere — the rate semantics now live in the basis label ("Per session" replaces "Individual sessions"). When a club has no membership name, the basis promotes to the primary line, collapsing the box to the trip Cost-box shape. Applied identically to all three instances: active single box, radio cards, and the full-state single box.
Rationale: With the trip Cost box and the club cost box both on screen across the prototype, the club box read heavier because it was doing card-work (semantic label + unit suffix) in a summary slot, while trips read as a clean "Cost / £45". Unifying them on one grid — text block left, bare amount right, basis carried in text never as a suffix — makes the two features feel like one system. The school's name has to own the primary line (not be demoted to a subtitle) because it carries scheduling weight a parent chooses on ("Tuesdays only"); it's also the better axis to disambiguate Football's repeated per-day periods than the basis would be. Evolves the earlier [[project_membership_period_labels]] rule, which showed the basis as a subtitle for daily only — it now shows for termly too, as the qualifier beneath the name. Half-term was ruled out as a distinct basis (the data model is binary daily/termly; half-term is only a session-date note).

[2026-06-10] Clubs — per-option (per-period) full states + option-aware race-at-commit
Decision: Fullness is now a property of the booking OPTION, not just the club — a club can have some options full while others are bookable (e.g. Football: Tuesday full, Wednesday open). Two surfaces handle it:
(1) Detail-page option stack: a full option renders as a NON-selectable row (no radio, muted text, grey "Full" tag where the price/radio would be), and full options sort to the bottom so actionable choices lead. Available options keep the normal selectable radio card. Selection can never land on a full option. The club itself stays bookable (footer CTA present), and stays bookable in browse — only a fully-full club shows the club-level Full state.
(2) Race-at-commit now has two variants. If the option you committed just filled but siblings remain → "option" sheet: titled by the option ("Wednesdays only just filled up"), body "It was taken while you were booking, but other options are still available." (vague on purpose — the surviving sibling may be a different basis on the same day, not another day), primary action "Choose another option" (records that period full, clears selection, returns to the stack with it now flagged Full). If it was the last bookable option → existing "club" sheet ("{club} just filled up" / "Browse other clubs"). The X/scrim resolves to the same action as the sheet's scope.
Data model: per-period `full: true` (static, school-marked) and `raceFull: true` (fills at the commit tap) on period objects; new `racedPeriodIds` state tracks options that raced full this session (distinct from `raceFilledIds`, which tracks whole clubs gone). `isPeriodFull(p) = p.full || racedPeriodIds.has(p.id)`. Demo wiring: Football (id 5) Tuesday periods `full`, Wednesday-termly `raceFull`; its club-level `raceOnCommit` was removed (now period-driven). Choir (id 18) keeps club-level `raceOnCommit` → single option → "club" variant.
Rationale: All-or-nothing club fullness was unrealistic for multi-option clubs — a parent told "full" when only their preferred day went would be misinformed, and ejecting them to browse when the same club still has space is the wrong exit. Keeping the full option visible (not hidden) follows the same legibility rule as full clubs in browse: the parent got a fixed email list and needs to reconcile it against live availability. Non-selectable row over a greyed/disabled radio honours the no-disabled-states rule (a radio implies a choice that no longer exists). Naming the race sheet by the OPTION and routing back INTO the club (vs. out to browse) matches the new name-primary cost-box hierarchy and keeps the parent in the flow. Builds on the race-at-commit plan ([[project_race_at_commit_plan]]) and the full-states legibility model ([[project_full_states]]).
